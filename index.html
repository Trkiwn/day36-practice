Intermediate

Match, Translate, and Judge

~24 min

A new passage, a keyword drill, and real-vs-trap judgment calls.

1.Read the passage, then match each statement to a paragraph.Matching
A) Lacquerware production in Vietnam dates back over two thousand years, with early artisans using resin from the sơn tree to coat wooden objects for protection against humidity and insects. For centuries, the craft remained largely utilitarian, producing simple bowls, trays, and ceremonial items.

B) In the early twentieth century, the École des Beaux-Arts de l'Indochine in Hanoi transformed lacquer from a craft into a fine art form. Students combined traditional Vietnamese techniques with French artistic training, experimenting with eggshell inlay, gold leaf, and multiple layers of polishing to create paintings rather than purely functional objects.

C) Today, Vietnamese lacquer paintings are exhibited in galleries worldwide, and the technique continues to evolve as contemporary artists blend traditional methods with modern subject matter. However, mastering the craft still requires years of apprenticeship, since each piece can take months to complete due to the many layers of lacquer and drying time required.
1. A specific school is credited with turning lacquer into an art form.
2. The material originally used to protect wooden items came from a tree.
3. Finished artworks can take a long time to produce because of a slow drying process.
4. Vietnamese lacquer art is now displayed internationally.
2.For each statement, identify the single best keyword or phrase to scan for first.Keyword Doctor
"The temple was rebuilt following a fire in 1952."
"An environmental group criticized the new factory's emissions."
"Dr. Nguyen Van A led the research team."
3.For each pair, judge whether the candidate sentence is a genuine match or a one-word trap.Real Match or Trap?
Statement: "The company's profits doubled last year."
Candidate sentence from passage: "Last year, the company launched three new products."
Statement: "Researchers found a surprising link between diet and sleep quality."
Candidate sentence from passage: "The study revealed an unexpected connection between what people eat and how well they sleep."
Advanced

Independent Matching & Diagnosis

~16 min

No hints this time -- a full passage with both traps built in, plus a reverse-engineering challenge.

1.Read the passage, then match each statement to a paragraph. Note: one paragraph matches nothing, and one paragraph matches twice.Matching
A) Urban beekeeping has grown rapidly in cities such as London, New York, and Hanoi over the past fifteen years, as residents install hives on rooftops and in community gardens. Enthusiasts argue that keeping bees in urban areas supports pollination for city parks and gardens.

B) However, not everyone welcomes the trend. Some scientists warn that a high concentration of managed honeybee hives in a small urban area can actually compete with wild native bee species for limited flowers, potentially harming biodiversity rather than helping it.

C) City governments have responded differently to this debate. Several municipalities now require hobbyist beekeepers to register their hives, while others have introduced subsidized training programs to encourage responsible practices.

D) Despite the controversy, demand for urban beekeeping courses continues to rise, and many participants report that the hobby has deepened their interest in gardening and environmental issues more broadly.
1. Keeping bees in cities may actually damage other bee populations.
2. People who take up this hobby often become more interested in environmental topics generally.
3. Local authorities now sometimes require beekeepers to officially record their hives.
4. A financial incentive exists in some areas to promote safer beekeeping practices.
Which paragraph letter did you not use at all?
2.A student answered this question incorrectly. Explain the trap they fell into.Diagnose the Error
Statement: "The hobby has become extremely popular in three specific cities."
Student's answer: Paragraph B, because "it also mentions bees in cities."
3.Reverse-engineer a statement: write ONE new, original statement that would correctly match Paragraph D above.Build a Statement
Application

Full Task: Complete Matching Set

~12 min
A) Night markets have long been a fixture of urban life across Southeast Asia, with roots stretching back to street vendors who began setting up stalls after sunset to avoid the daytime heat. Today, cities such as Bangkok, Taipei, and Ho Chi Minh City are famous for night markets that draw both locals and tourists.

B) Beyond food, modern night markets increasingly feature live music, handicraft stalls, and pop-up fashion boutiques, transforming what was once a purely practical shopping option into a form of evening entertainment.

C) Local governments have taken notice of the economic value these markets generate, with some cities now actively promoting night markets as part of broader tourism campaigns, while providing vendors with improved sanitation facilities and designated parking areas.

D) Critics, however, point out that rising rents in popular tourist areas have pushed many longtime vendors to relocate to less convenient locations, raising concerns about the loss of authentic local culture in favor of markets designed primarily for visitors.
1.Match each statement to a paragraph (A-D). Some paragraphs are used more than once; at least one is not used at all.Matching
1. Night markets originally developed partly as a way to escape high daytime temperatures.
2. Several well-known cities are specifically named as popular night market destinations.
3. Authorities have begun incorporating night markets into official tourism strategies.
4. Vendors have improved access to basic infrastructure thanks to government support.
5. Increasing costs have forced some long-established vendors out of prime locations.
6. Some observers worry that catering mainly to tourists threatens genuine local traditions.
